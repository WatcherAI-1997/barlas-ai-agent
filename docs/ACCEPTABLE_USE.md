# Barlas AI Agent acceptable-use baseline

Barlas AI Agent may be used for legitimate research, writing, software work,
operations, document processing, and authorized automation. It must not be used
to:

- access accounts, devices, networks, or data without authorization;
- create, deploy, or conceal malware, credential theft, destructive payloads,
  phishing, spam, or denial-of-service activity;
- bypass approval gates, quotas, organizational permissions, or audit controls;
- expose personal data, connector secrets, private prompts, or workspace files
  without a lawful basis and the necessary authority;
- automate fraud, impersonation, harassment, discrimination, exploitation,
  trafficking, child sexual abuse material, weapons, or physical harm;
- make unreviewed high-impact decisions in medicine, law, finance, employment,
  education, housing, insurance, policing, or critical infrastructure;
- violate intellectual-property, privacy, export-control, sanctions, consumer,
  or other applicable laws.

Owners may suspend access, revoke sessions and connectors, preserve relevant
audit evidence, and notify competent parties when reasonably necessary to
protect people, the service, or legal compliance. This baseline must be adapted
to the operator's jurisdiction before public release.
